## Where to sell (ranked by net price)

1. **Direct-to-consumer** (best margin): farm-gate, restaurants/hotels in Boquete & Volcán, WhatsApp/Instagram "organic box" with a weekly delivery day, local ferias (Feria de las Flores y el Café). Captures ~B/.2.00–2.50/mazo.
2. **Merca David / Mercado Público de la Cadena de Frío (David)** — nearest wholesale, lowest freight. Producers sell directly (solicitud → spot assigned by rubro). Mayoristas until 3:00 pm.
3. **Merca Panamá (Ciudad de Panamá)** — highest volume but freight + ACPTA module-access friction. Via centros post-cosecha (Volcán / Cerro Punta).
4. **IMA** — bulk purchases + agrotiendas/agroferias. Live prices via SIPAN. Contact: informacion@ima.gob.pa, +507 501-4513.
5. **Supermarkets & distributors** — Riba Smith, Super 99, El Rey, Xtra, mostly via intermediaries (Cultivos Selectos, ANEF, Frulesa, Vegetales Fresquita, Janson Farms). Requires calibration, packing, traceability, consistent volume.
6. **ACPTA** (Asociación de la Comunidad Productora de Tierras Altas) — collective bargaining, group transport, market-access leverage. President: Lorenzo Jiménez.

## Earnings estimate — 2,000 m² organic, sold as conventional

Assumptions: 0.2 ha; organic rabanito yield 8–15 t/ha (central 12 t/ha); ~20 g/root; ~82% marketable; 11 radishes/mazo; B/.1.08/mazo; 45–70-day cycle.

| Scenario | Monthly gross (wholesale) |
|---|---|
| Conservative (8 t/ha, 70 d, 80%) | ≈ **B/.2,700** |
| **Central (12 t/ha, 55 d)** | ≈ **B/.5,300** |
| Optimistic (15 t/ha, 45 d, 85%) | ≈ **B/.8,300** |

Central case detail: 2.4 t/cycle → ~120,000 radishes → ~98,400 marketable → ~8,945 mazos → B/.9,660/cycle → **~B/.5,300/month**.

### Reality check
- Organic inputs are essentially free (compost, bokashi, LABs, vermicast — made on-farm). Cash inputs are tiny (seed).
- **Labor dominates**: hand weeding, thinning, harvest, washing, bunching. Bunching ≈ 30–60 mazos/hr → 150–300 hrs/cycle just bunching.
- At B/.1.08/mazo wholesale, bunching pays ~B/.30–60/hr — below field-labor wages.
- **Net ≈ B/.3,000–4,000/month** after paying labor; if family/self labor, it's ~B/.5,300/month of labor income.

### Levers
1. **Sell direct** at B/.2.00–2.50/mazo → same land grosses **~B/.10,000–12,000/month** (2–3× wholesale).
2. **Don't dedicate all 2,000 m² to rábano** — use it as a 45-day rotation/filler between higher-value crops.
3. **Charge the organic premium** — selling as conventional gives it away.
