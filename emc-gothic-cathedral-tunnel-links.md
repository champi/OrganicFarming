## Key Takeaway: They Don't Both Use 1" EMT

**Eliot Coleman's** design uses **1" EMT** as the main structural hoops. This is the classic "bend your own greenhouse from electrical conduit" design. The 1" EMT hoops slide into 1-3/8" top rail base sockets.

**Johnny's #7300 Gothic Cathedral Tunnel** uses **1-3/8" chain-link fence top rail** for the main bows — a heavier, stiffer material. EMT is only used for secondary components (collar ties, roll-ups, braces). The Gothic peak shape requires a special bender (#7300) with a peak jig.

## Which Design for Volcán?

For cherry tomato rain protection in Volcán (no snow, but wind + wet season):

| Design | Suitable? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Eliot Coleman 12'6" Quonset** | ✅ Yes | Simpler. 1" EMT available at Novey. Less tooling needed. 12'6" fits four 30" beds. |
| **Johnny's #7300 14' Gothic** | Overkill | Needs 1-3/8" top rail (harder to source in Panama). Requires #7300 bender ($200+). Gothic shape is for snow — unnecessary in Volcán. |

For a starter tunnel in Volcán, the **Eliot Coleman 1" EMT design** is the practical choice. Simpler materials, simpler bending, proven for 15+ years at Four Season Farm.
