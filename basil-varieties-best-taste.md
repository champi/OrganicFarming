## Key Notes

- **Hydroponic vs. Soil**: Genovese varieties develop their best essential oil profile
  in soil. Johnny's own catalog recommends different, more uniform varieties for
  hydroponic production — confirming what you experienced with the hydroponic Genovese
  not tasting as good.
- **Basil crosses freely**: All basil varieties are insect-pollinated and cross within
  800-2,640 feet. If saving seed, grow only one variety or use isolation cages.
- **Seed viability**: 5 years when stored cool and dry (not refrigerated).
- **Sources**: NC State Extension Master Gardener trials (2021-2022), URI variety
  trials (2019), University of Maryland Extension (2020), Johnny's Selected Seeds
  catalog/professional trials, SAIS Sementi breeding program documentation.
