## Part 2 — How many families of (manual) seeding machines exist?

The literature groups machines two complementary ways: **by seeding/placement method** and **by metering mechanism**. Combined, there are roughly **8–10 families** of manually operated machines.

### A. Classification by seeding method (Murray et al. 2006; Baidu Baike; Shandong Univ. courseware)

| # | Family | What it does | Typical crops |
|---|--------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Broadcast seeder** (撒播机) | Scatters seed over an area; no row/depth control | Grass, pasture, cover crops, lawn |
| 2 | **Drill / row seeder** (条播机) | Drops a continuous seed stream in a furrow (mass flow, random in-row) | Small grains: wheat, barley, oats, rye; forage |
| 3 | **Hill-drop / hill seeder** (穴播机 / 点播机) | Drops 1–several seeds per hole at fixed spacing | Corn, cotton, soybean, sunflower, peanut, beans |
| 4 | **Precision / singulated seeder** (精量播种机) | Places single seeds almost equidistant | High-value small seed: sugar beet, carrot, vegetables, flower seed |
| 5 | **Dibble / dibbler** | Makes a hole, drops seed by hand (no meter) | Large seed, transplants, seedlings |
| 6 | **Specialized** (no-till 免耕, plastic-mulch 铺膜, plug-tray 穴盘, rice drum) | Task-specific | No-till grains, mulch crops, nursery trays, pregerminated rice |

### B. Classification by metering mechanism (most useful for seed matching)

| # | Manual machine family | Metering type | Brand/examples | Best seed |
|---|----------------------|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Jab / stick planter** (matraca / hand dibbler) | No continuous meter — poke & release 1–3 seeds per jab | Stand 'n Plant, Easy-Plant Jab-Type, matraca | Large seed: corn, beans, sunflower, squash; works through mulch/plastic |
| 2 | **Hand-push/pull wheel drill (multi-nozzle drum)** | Rotating drum/duckbill nozzles, 1–2 seeds per nozzle | Chinese 手推式播种机 (the 10-nozzle), Haudin/Kaisen | Corn, soybean, peanut, mung bean, cotton, sunflower, dry grain |
| 3 | **Gravity-fed plate seeder** | Interchangeable vertical/horizontal plates with holes | Earthway 1001-B, Hoss, Jang TD, Cole Planet Jr | Large + some small seed; flat seeds |
| 4 | **Gravity-fed roller / seed-wheel seeder** | Interchangeable rollers with recessed cells | Jang JP series, Glaser wheel-hoe attachment | Small → medium seed (best singulation of small seed) |
| 5 | **Belt seeder** | Rubber belt with indentations | Stanhay | Small seed, precision row crops |
| 6 | **Pinpoint / multi-row pin seeder** | Tiny fixed holes, densely spaced | Johnny's Four-Row / Six-Row Pinpoint | Dense small seed: salad mix, baby greens, carrot, radish |
| 7 | **Hand broadcast / whirl spreader** | Centrifugal or hand-crank | Ev-n-Spred, Scotts | Grass, cover crop, fertilizer, small-seed broadcast |
| 8 | **Vacuum / pneumatic precision seeder** (now small battery/manual units) | Vacuum singulates seed on a plate | Monosem, Stanhay, Wizard; Chinese 气吸式 plug seeders | Any seed — gold standard; plug-tray nursery seeding |
| 9 | **Drum seeder (wet/rice)** | Rotating drum drops pregerminated seed | Rice drum seeders | Pregerminated rice (wet fields) |
| 10 | **Finger-pickup seeder** | Metal fingers scoop seed (mostly tractor) | John Deere 7000 | Large round seed (corn, soybean) |

> **Short answer:** ~**5 method-families** (broadcast, drill, hill-drop, precision, dibble) or ~**8–10 mechanism-families** counting metering type. FAO simplifies to just **two practical manual families** — *hand seeders/drills* and *jab planters* — distinguished by seedbed requirement (hand seeders need tilled beds; jab planters work in untilled/mulched soil).

## Part 3 — Which machine for which seed

Core principle (all sources agree):

> **All seeders handle spherical/round seed well. The metering part (plate hole, roller cell, nozzle, finger) must be sized to the seed's diameter, and its shape must accommodate the seed's shape.**

The four variables that decide machine/plate/roller: **seed size, seed shape, desired in-row spacing, and seedbed condition.**

### By seed size class

| Seed size | Examples | Recommended machine / plate / roller |
|-----------|----------|--------------------------------------|
| **Very large (>12 mm)** | Peanut, jumbo pea, lima bean, large bean, field corn | Jab planter; multi-nozzle hand-push seeder; plate seeders w/ large plates (Hoss #6 @ .56", Earthway Lima .625"/15.9mm); Jang TD |
| **Large (8–12 mm)** | Corn, bean, pea, soybean, popcorn | Plate seeder (Hoss #4/#5, Earthway Sweet Corn .375"/9.5mm, Beans .450"); 10-nozzle hand-push seeder; Jang TD corn plate (8mm) |
| **Medium (4–8 mm)** | Okra, beet, chard, cucumber, small melon, sunflower | Earthway Beet/Okra .250"/6.3mm; Jang G/R/LJ rollers (7.5–9mm); Hoss #3/#8 |
| **Small (2–4 mm)** | Carrot, lettuce, cabbage, onion, broccoli, mustard, turnip, radish, spinach | **Roller seeders** (Jang JP: X/Y/YX 2.5–4mm); Pinpoint seeders; vacuum precision. *Plate seeders (Earthway) struggle here* |
| **Very small / fine (<2 mm)** | Amaranth, watercress, claytonia, wild arugula, basil | Jang YYX/YYJ rollers (1.5–2mm); vacuum; broadcast |
| **Pelleted / coated** | Pelleted carrot, lettuce, onion (uniform, e.g. #13 = 13/64") | Works in most; match hole to *pellet* diameter, not raw seed |

### By seed shape

| Seed shape | Examples | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Round / spherical** | Brassica, radish, turnip, peas, soybean | Easiest — any meter works |
| **Flat / disc** | Pumpkin, squash, sunflower, watermelon, cantaloupe | Need **flat-seed plates/rollers** with wide, shallow cells (Earthway Cucumber/Pumpkin .312"/8mm; Jang TD blank drilled wide) |
| **Long / narrow / oblong** | Cucumber, parsnip, burdock, fennel, carrot | Need **shaped holes** — Jang "J" half-moon rollers; XY cross-shaped rollers; Earthway poor here |
| **Irregular / winged** | Some carrot, lettuce | Cross-shaped (XY/XYY) Jang rollers; or pelleted seed |

### Key seed-plate/roller charts (from manufacturer literature)

**Earthway 1001-B** (11 plates):
- Sweet Corn → .375" (9.5mm); Beans → .450" (11.4mm); Peas → .562" (14.3mm); Lima → .625" (15.9mm)
- Beet → .250" (6.3mm); Okra/chard → .250"; Carrot → .125" (3.2mm); Radish/leek/spinach → .175" (4.4mm)
- Broccoli/cabbage/mustard fine → .100" (2.5mm); Cucumber/pumpkin/squash flat → .312" (8mm)

**Hoss** (6 standard + 4 optional):
- #1 broccoli/cabbage/turnip/mustard (.09") · #2 onion/small coated (.18×.10") · #3 okra/carrot/lettuce/chard/beet (.25") · #4 small sweet corn/popcorn (.50") · #5 small peas/beans (.37") · #6 medium beans/peas/large corn (.56") · #7 radish/leek/asparagus/spinach (.16") · #8 cucumber/watermelon (.41") · #9 lima (.60") · #10 squash/pumpkin (.66")

**Jang JP roller series** (2mm → 13.5mm holes):
- YX 2.5 → YYJ 3 → Y 3.5 → X 4 → F/M 5 → MM/MJ 6 → L 7 → LJ 7.5 → Q 8 → R/G 9 → B 10 → C 11 → AA 12 → A 13.5 mm
- Shaped holes: **J** (half-moon, cucumber), **N** (scoop, small beans/corn), **XY/XYY** (cross, carrot/lettuce)

**Jang TD** (large-seed plates): Bean 14mm, Corn 8mm, Pea 9.5mm, + blank plates drilled to custom size.

### Decision checklist

1. **Seedbed:** tilled & flat → any seeder; untilled/mulch/plastic → *jab planter* only (or the reinforced duckbill of the Chinese hand-push seeder).
2. **Seed size** → narrows to plate/roller/nozzle hole size.
3. **Seed shape** → round = anything; flat/oblong/irregular = shaped cells or pelleted seed.
4. **Desired spacing** → number of holes per plate/roller + gearing (Jang uses sprockets; Earthway fixed per plate).
5. **Singulation need** → exact single-seed placement needs **precision (roller/vacuum)**; if you'll thin anyway, a cheap **plate or drill** is fine (Parish & Bracy 2004: Earthway is "acceptable but not precision", ~1/10th the cost).
6. **Crop density** → dense small seed (salad mix, baby greens) → *pinpoint/multi-row or roller*; wide-spaced large seed → *jab or plate*.

## Practical takeaway for the farm

- The **10-nozzle hand-push seeder** (family 2, multi-nozzle drum) handles **large dry seeds** (corn, soybean, peanut, mung bean, cotton, sunflower) at 1–2 seeds/hole — and is **not** suited to fine seed (carrot, lettuce, cabbage).
- For **small/fine vegetable seed** (carrot, lettuce, brassica, onion) → **roller seeder (Jang JP)** or **pinpoint seeder**.
- For **flat seed** (pumpkin, squash, sunflower) → specific **flat-seed plates** (Earthway/Hoss).
- For **planting through mulch / untilled beds** → **jab planter / matraca**.
