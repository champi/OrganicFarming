# Potassium Sulfate (Sulfato de Potasio, SOP, 0-0-50) — Where to Buy in Panama

**Goal:** ~60 g for 4 zucchini (15 g/plant/season). Buy a pound loose if possible. Big bags are 25–50 kg.
**Ask for:** *sulfato de potasio* (granulado or soluble), 50% K₂O + 17–18% S. **Not** *cloruro/muriato de potasio* (0-0-60), *Sul-Po-Mag/K-Mag*, or *nitrato de potasio*.
