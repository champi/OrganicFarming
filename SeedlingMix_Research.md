## 1. Component-by-Component Research

### 1.1 Biochar in Seedling Mixes

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| 5-10% v/v stimulates germination, speeds emergence by 1-3 days | MDPI Agronomy 2019 (cabbage), MDPI 2020 (lettuce) |
| At 20%+, emergence declines in many crops | Multiple MDPI studies 2019-2025 |
| 70-80% biochar + 20-30% vermicompost matched/exceeded commercial mix for 11 of 15 species | MDPI Applied Sciences 2019 |
| Pre-washing biochar increased radicle length +50% and germination index +70% in basil | MDPI Plants 2023 |
| Wood biochar (beech/spruce/pine) at 700°C consistently best performer | MDPI Agronomy 2019 |
| Biochar reduces EC of high-salinity mixes (critical buffer effect) | MDPI Agronomy 2020 |
| Low rates of biochar can have a stimulatory effect on germination | MDPI Horticulturae 2023 |

**Safe range for seedlings: 5-10% v/v. Above 15-20% = emergence suppression.**

### 1.8 Eggshell / Water-Soluble Calcium (WCA)

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Eggshell = 94-97% CaCO₃ (calcium carbonate) — identical to agricultural lime | MDPI Agronomy 2024 |
| **Not water-soluble** unless ground to <75μm or acid-extracted | Multiple studies |
| Particle size determines release: whole (5-10yr) → coarse (1-3yr) → fine (6-18mo) → powder (weeks) | GrowPerma 2026 |
| Finely ground eggshell matches aglime performance for pH and Ca | MDPI Agronomy 2024, Nigeria 2014 |
| 15g/pot increased mustard height +36%, leaves +70%, leaf width +171% (but at pH 5.5 acting as lime) | Malaysia 2025 |
| 10% eggshell powder improved Vigna growth; 15% caused toxicity | Publiscience 2021 |
| For seedlings in hydrogel: best at 10:1 (agar:eggshell) — higher ratios physically restricted roots | JMANS 2026 |
| KNF WCA (vinegar extraction): immediately plant-available calcium acetate | U. Hawaii SA-10 |
| WCA used at 1:500-1:1000 dilution for seedlings | Multiple KNF sources |
| "Seeds deficient in calcium generally have poor germination rates and produce abnormal, weak seedlings" | U. Hawaii KNF research |
| WCA in seed-soaking solution with OHN + FPJ + BRV + FAA | KNF protocol |

**For seedling mix: raw powder at 0.5-1% volume (2-3 tbsp per 10L) for slow Ca + pH buffer. WCA at 1:1000 as seed soak or soil drench for immediate Ca.**

## 2. The Living Soil Paradigm (No Pasteurization)

### 2.1 Evidence Against Sterilization

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Epic Gardening / multiple studies | "Heat-sterilizing compost is not recommended... overheating soil can cause phytotoxicity that prevents germination" |
| BMC Plant Biology 2025 | No growth advantage from heat-treating vermicompost; biological activity enhanced seedling media |
| Elmdirt / university ag studies | 79% better germination with living beneficial microbes vs. sterile mix |
| PNW Disease Handbook | "Sterilized or fumigated soil can be re-colonized by damping-off fungi" — sterility is temporary |
| Journal of Plant Pathology 2022 | Organic farming soils harbor disease-suppressive bacterial communities (Burkholderiales) |
| Properly made compost reaches 130-140°F naturally — kills pathogens, spares beneficials | Cornell / Epic Gardening |

**Core insight: Damping-off is prevented by competitive exclusion, not sterility. When beneficial microbes colonize first, pathogens have no niche.**

## 3. Korean Natural Farming (KNF) for Seedlings

### 3.1 KNF Seedling Protocol

```
PHASE 1: PREPARE LIVING SOIL (1 week before sowing)
├── IMO#4 (Indigenous Microorganisms stage 4) at 5% volume
├── Water in with SOS (Soil Treatment Solution):
│   ├── LAB 1:1000 (anti-damping-off)
│   ├── FPJ 1:500 (microbe food + growth hormones)
│   ├── OHN 1:1000 (antifungal)
│   ├── BRV 1:500 (nutrient unlock)
│   └── Seawater 1:30 (trace minerals)
└── Let sit 7 days for biology to stabilize

PHASE 2: SEED TREATMENT
├── SSES (Seed Soak): OHN + FPJ + BRV + WCA (very dilute)
└── LAB mist on soil surface

PHASE 3: POST-GERMINATION
├── WCa 1:1000 (calcium for strong cell walls)
├── FPJ foliar (growth hormones)
├── LAB mist (ongoing damping-off prevention)
└── NO FAA (nitrogen) until 3rd true leaf
```

### 3.2 KNF Inputs

| Input | Role | Dilution |
|---|---|---|
| IMO#4 | Soil biology foundation | 5% volume in mix, 150kg/0.1ha field |
| LAB | Anti-damping-off, soil conditioner | 1:1000 |

| OHN | Antifungal medicine | 1:1000 |

## 4. JADAM (Ultra-Low-Cost) for Seedlings

### 4.1 JADAM vs KNF

| Aspect | KNF | JADAM |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Han-Kyu Cho (father) | Youngsang Cho (son, founded 1991) |
| Sugar | Heavy use (brown sugar 1:1) | None — uses potatoes |
| Fermentation | Aerobic (open, shade) | Anaerobic (sealed containers) |
| Microbe selection | "White mold only" | All microbes — no separation |
| Complexity | IMO 1→2→3→4 (weeks) | JMS (2-3 days) |
| Fertilizer | FPJ, FFJ, FAA, OHN (multiple) | JLF — one process |
| Cost | Moderate | $100/acre/year |
| Philosophy | Precise nutrient cycling | "Make your soil like leaf mold" |

### 4.2 JMS (JADAM Microbial Solution)

```
INGREDIENTS:
├── Water (rainwater or dechlorinated) — 10-20L
├── Leaf mold soil — handful (the starter)
├── Boiled potatoes — 1-2, mashed (the food)
└── Sea salt — ~330ml per 20L (minerals)

PROCESS:
1. Fill bucket with water, add sea salt
2. Put leaf mold + mashed potato in mesh bag
3. Submerge, massage gently
4. Cover loosely (anaerobic) — 36-72 hours at 18-25°C
5. Bubbles/foam forming circular disc on surface = done
6. Use immediately while foam is active

APPLICATION:
├── Seed starting: dilute 1:100, saturate potting mix
├── Soil drench: dilute 1:10
├── Foliar: dilute 1:100
└── Never apply undiluted — can burn roots
```

**Tip from Korean farmers**: Don't wash the mesh bag between batches. Leaving residue and adding fresh leaf mold on top results in much faster, more vigorous cultures (up to 5 consecutive uses).

### 4.3 JLF (JADAM Liquid Fertilizer)

```
INGREDIENTS:
├── Any plant material (weeds, grass, leaves) — fill 50% of container
├── Water — fill to top
└── Handful of leaf mold soil — starter

PROCESS:
1. Seal tight (anaerobic)
2. Ferment 7-10 days in shade
3. Dilute 1:20 general, 1:30 for seedlings
```

### 4.4 JS (JADAM Sulfur) — 10L Batch

```
INGREDIENTS (add in exact order):
├── Sulfur powder (99% pure) ........... 2.5 kg
├── Phyllite/rock dust (optional) ...... 0.05 kg
├── Red clay powder (optional) ......... 0.05 kg
├── Sea salt ........................... 0.25 kg
├── Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) ............ 2.0 kg
├── Water (first addition) ............. 5.0 L
└── Water (second addition) ............ 3.2 L

SAFETY: Goggles, gloves, long sleeves, mask, OUTDOORS ONLY.
Container: heat-resistant HDPE/PE or stainless steel. NEVER aluminum.

PROCESS (20-30 min):
1. Dry ingredients in order → add all 5L water at once
2. Stir continuously with wooden stick
3. Temperature reaches 80°C+ in 3-4 min (NaOH + water = exothermic)
4. Sulfur melts at ~80°C — keep stirring ~20 min until no granules remain
5. Have 1-2L extra water ready if it starts to boil over
6. Add second water (3.2L), stir, cover, settle 24-48 hrs
7. Pour clear upper liquid into PET/HDPE bottles
8. Filter sediment through cloth

USAGE:
├── Dilute 0.5-2L JS in 500L water (1:250 to 1:1000)
├── Greenhouse: start 0.5L/500L | Open field: start 1L/500L
├── Only use when disease is present — continuous use stunts growth
├── Grapes, persimmons, walnuts, squash = sulfur-sensitive (use half rate)
├── Always mix with JWA (JADAM Wetting Agent)
└── Cost: ~$1/L to make. No expiry. Store above -5°C.

CONTROLS: Anthracnose, powdery mildew, downy mildew, black spot, 
pear rust, canker, most fungal/bacterial diseases.
Pathogens do NOT develop resistance. USDA organic approved.
```

### 4.5 JADAM Seedling Approach

```
JADAM SEEDLING SYSTEM (minimalist):
├── Base: LEAF MOLD SOIL (from forest) — that's basically it
├── Water with JMS 1:100 before sowing
├── Seed treatment: JS 100x soak (38-72 hrs cold, or 60°C 10 min)
├── Seedling dip before transplant: leaf mold water
└── Ongoing: JLF 1:30 once true leaves appear

Core philosophy: "Let us get rid of the compost method!"
— Use raw leaf mold directly. Nature's seed-starting medium.
```

### 5.2 Recipe A: Balanced (Start Here)

```
For 10L total:
├── 40% Pine needle leaf mold ........... 4.0L

├── 15% Vermicast ...................... 1.5L
```

## 6. Damping-Off Prevention Without Sterilization

| Method | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| LAB (Lactic Acid Bacteria) 1:1000 | Lowers pH locally, produces antibiotics, outcompetes fungi |
| Competitive exclusion | Living mix already full of beneficials — no room for Pythium/Rhizoctonia |
| Good drainage | Biochar + leaf mold structure = aeration. Pathogens love waterlogged soil |
| Air circulation | Small fan or breeze — prevents humidity buildup |
| Don't overwater | Surface should dry slightly between waterings |
| Proper temperature | 20-25°C for most vegetables. >30°C stresses seedlings |
| Trichoderma | Naturally in healthy leaf mold/compost; aggressive colonizer |
| 7-day pre-incubation | Allows beneficials to establish before seeds go in |

## 7. Quality Control Targets

| Parameter | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| EC (salinity) | <2.0 dS/m | Above this, emergence drops sharply |
| pH | 5.5-7.0 | Most vegetables germinate well here |
| Total porosity | >70% | Roots need oxygen |
| Moisture at sowing | "Wrung-out sponge" | Seeds need consistent moisture, not saturation |

**Bioassay**: If you don't have meters, sow 10 fast seeds (radish/lettuce). >80% emergence = safe mix.

---

## 8. Key Interactions Summary

1. **Biochar buffers EC**: Reduces salinity of vermicast and urine, critical for germination safety
2. **Biochar needs nutrients**: Alone has low N/P. Urine-charging solves this.
3. **Urine-biochar is slow-release**: ~50% of urine-N available in first 5 days, rest slower
4. **Vermicast + biochar synergy**: BC moderates VC's high EC, VC provides what BC lacks
5. **Pine needle mold is fungal-dominant**: More acidic, slower decomposition. Vermicast + biochar balance this.
6. **Native soil brings AMF**: Your local mycorrhizae — cannot be bought, must come from your land
7. **7-day pre-incubation is mandatory**: Lets biology stabilize, EC settle, beneficials colonize biochar
