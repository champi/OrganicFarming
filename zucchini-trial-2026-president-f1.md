# Zucchini Trial 2026 — 4 × 'President F1'

> Sown **16 Sep 2026**, Volcán. Rationale and sources: [zucchini-nutrition-guide.md](zucchini-nutrition-guide.md) (§7 for mycorrhizae).

## Setup

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plants | 4 × President F1, ~1 m² each |
| ~5 Oct (≈19 DAS) | Vermicast + biochar charged with aged urine + 5 g/L Epsom (slow struvite P; AMF-friendly) |
| Mycorrhizae | Great White **at sowing (16 Sep)** → colonization established ~mid-Oct |
| Soil | Andisol, pH ~6.6 (no P/K/Ca test yet; assume medium) |
| Expected first harvest | ~5–15 Nov (50–60 DAS) |

## Per-plant inputs for the season

| Input | Amount per plant | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aged urine + ≤5 g/L Epsom | ~1.5 L total, weekly | Shake the jug; dilute 1:4–1:5; water in. Epsom at this rate does not hurt Ca. |
| Bone ash, **lightly** acidified → vermicast | **~15 g, once** | 4-plant batch: 60 g bone ash + 4–5 g citric acid + 60–90 mL water, ≤24 h, then into ~0.5–1 L vermicast, buffer 2–3 days |
| Potassium sulfate (SOP) | 3 × 5 g | Chloride-free; same day as urine is OK |
| Great White top-up (optional) | ~½ tsp (~2 g) | Only if you have product to spare: side band 10–15 cm from stem, 5–8 cm deep |
| Gypsum (*yeso agrícola*) | 10–20 g, **only if** Ca symptoms persist | pH-neutral, no P |
| Wood ash (optional) | ≤30–40 g per season | Only if pH stays <6.9; ≥3 days from urine |

**Bone-ash form:** Great White went in at sowing, so use **lightly acidified** bone ash (6–8% citric, ≤24 h) on ~Oct 21, in pockets separate from the biochar.
