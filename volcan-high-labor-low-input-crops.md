# High-Labor, Low-Input, Easy-to-Sell Crops — Volcán Andisol

*Research findings — 29 Aug 2026*

## Context

Constraint set (flipped from prior research):
- **Cheap labor available** (~$13/day workers everywhere).
- **Easy-to-sell / liquid market** preferred (commodity demand at Merca Panamá, supermarkets, restaurants).
- **Lower organic input** desired (minimize fertility/input effort and cost).

Conditions: Volcán 1,300–1,500 m, 16–27 °C, 2,400–3,600 mm rain, Andisol (pH 5.7–6.5, high organic matter, high P/K/Mg, well-drained).

## Key principle: nitrogen demand drives input cost

The main "organic input" is fertility, and the main fertility driver is nitrogen. Lower-N crops = lower input.

| N demand (lbs N/acre) | Crops |
|---|---|
| ~30–60 (lowest) | dry bean, cowpea, **snap bean**, pea, lima bean (legumes fix their own N) |
| 60–80 | sweet potato, **radish**, turnip, okra |
| 90–120 | carrot, garlic, **cilantro**, lettuce, leafy greens |
| 150–200+ (heaviest) | onion, potato, cabbage, broccoli, tomato, celery |

Sources: Cornell Vegetable Guidelines, Oklahoma State soil-test interpretations, K+S potassium-demand ranking, Poland N-demand ranking.

## Tier 1 — Best fit

### 1. Vainita / green beans / habichuela (*Phaseolus vulgaris*) — top pick
- **Lowest input**: legume; fixes its own N via *Rhizobium*. Cornell: ~40 lbs N/acre (vs 200 for potato). "Bella" variety bred for **low-fertility soil tolerance**.
- **Altitude fit**: optimal 1,200–2,100 m, 13–26 °C — Volcán dead-center.
- **Easy sell**: high constant demand; ~60-day cycle; "alto rendimiento, bajo costo de producción, alta demanda."
- **Labor-intensive in the cheap-labor way**: repeated hand-picking every 2–3 days over weeks (perfect for hired workers).
- **Yield/profit**: 13,000–15,000 kg pods/ha. Green/tierna beans are the profitable small-farm crop (staggered sowings for continuous supply).
- **Bonus**: leaves N in the soil for the next crop (rotation benefit).

### 2. Culantro (*Eryngium foetidum*, "recao") + cilantro (*Coriandrum sativum*)
- **Culantro = hidden gem for Panama**: the "other cilantro" — native, shade-tolerant (grows under trees), does not bolt, produces year-round, essential to Panamanian/Caribbean cooking. Premium price ($4–8/lb in ethnic markets). Low input.
- **Cilantro**: 30–55-day crop, ~120 lbs N/acre (short cycle = low total), multiple staggered crops/year, always in demand. Labor = cutting + bunching.
- **Both**: high turnover, guaranteed local demand, minimal input beyond residual andisol fertility. India data: 20–35% profit margins per cycle, daily cash flow off small plots.

### 3. Lettuce / leafy greens
- **Easiest to sell** (top-5 Cerro Punta crop; ~B/.0.89/lb national). Labor-intensive cut/wash/pack.
- **Input caveat**: N-hungry (~120 lbs N/acre) — "medium" input, not low. Manageable with on-farm compost/vermicast.

### 4. Carrot
- Staple, always sells, high volume; ~90 lbs N/acre (medium-low). Root crop = loose soil + thinning/harvest labor.

### 5. Guandú verde + arveja (peas)
- Legumes (fix N), national staples, easy to sell, harvest labor. Same logic as vainita.

## Tier 2 — Ultra-low input, lower value (rotation/filler)

- **Radish**: fertilizer "prácticamente innecesario" (practically unnecessary); 80 lbs N/acre, very low K, 25–35-day cycle. Ultra-low input but tiny value (~B/.1.08/mazo). Use as a fast cash-gap crop between main crops.
- **Sweet potato**: ~60 lbs N/acre, low input, but lower price and needs warmth (marginal at 1,400 m).

## The honest tension

- **Easiest to sell** (potato, onion, cabbage, tomato) = **heaviest feeders** (150–200+ lbs N/acre, disease-prone) — they are the reason Cerro Punta relies on heavy pesticide/fertilizer use.
- **Lowest input** (beans, peas, radish) = sell fine but smaller margin.
- **Sweet spot** (both goals): **vainita, culantro/cilantro, lettuce, carrot**.

## Recommendation

If "low input" dominates: **vainita + culantro + cilantro** (legumes and fast leafy herbs — near-zero fertility need, weekly sales, cheap labor converts into sellable product).

If "guaranteed volume / easy sell" dominates: **lettuce + carrot + onion**, fed with existing compost (higher input route).
