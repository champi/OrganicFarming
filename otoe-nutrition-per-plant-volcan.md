# Otoe (*Xanthosoma*) Nutrition: Per-Plant Doses of Our Inputs for Volcán

> **Literature review, Nov 2026.** How much urine, Epsom salt, biochar, SOP, bone ash, vermicast, Great White, rice husk and mulch to give each otoe plant on Volcán's Andisol.
> Growing basics (climate fit, spacing, *Pythium* root rot, harvest): [taro-otoe-cooking-growing-volcan.md](taro-otoe-cooking-growing-volcan.md). Bone ash and SOP method: [garden-crops-bone-ash-sop-doses.md](garden-crops-bone-ash-sop-doses.md).

**Assumptions:** ~1 m² per plant, a 10–12 month cycle, no soil test yet (Volcán Mg typically high). No trial has tested urine, bone ash or Great White on otoe. The per-plant numbers are converted from per-hectare trials of synthetic fertilizer and manure.

---

## 1. What one plant takes up

| Source | N | P | K | Mg | Ca | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vicente-Chandler 1982, Morada (purple), 1 × 0.7 m | **~9 g** | **~1 g** | **~11 g** | **~3.4 g** | ~1.8 g | 125/15/156/48/25 kg/ha; ~11.5 t/ha at 12 months |
| Goenaga & Chardón 1993, intensive management | 2–3× higher | up to ~5 g | up to ~25 g | ~4 g | ~6 g | 307/83/417/68/112 kg/ha; up to 34 t/ha |
| Jayapal 2020, Kerala, 0.75 × 0.75 m | ~4 g | ~0.7 g | ~6–8 g | — | — | Lower-yield site (~10–14 t/ha) |

- **N and K** uptake keeps rising until harvest. **P** rises slowly. **Mg and Ca** stop rising after ~5–6 months (Vicente-Chandler 1982).
- **K is the yield driver.** Soil K correlated with cormel size (Gwan 2025), and potash gave the best yield in an Akwa Ibom manure trial. Otoe's K need is high (Ivahupa 2006).
- Kerala found otoe disorders were mostly acidity-driven K, Ca and Mg deficiencies (Susan John & Suja 2012).

## 2. Per-plant doses

| Input | Dose per plant | When / where | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bone ash** | 15–20 g at planting + 15 g at 3–4 months | Stirred through a ring of soil 20–30 cm from the plant | ~2–3× P uptake, lowered for AMF (unchanged from the doses sheet) |
| **SOP** | **~20 g per season: 4 × 5 g** | Months 1, 3, 5, 7, dry, scratched into the ring | 60–70% of ~11 g K uptake. PR: 150 kg K₂O/ha ≈ 21 g SOP/plant; Kerala 150 kg K₂O/ha ≈ 17 g |
| **Urine (aged)** | **~1.5–2 L per season**, diluted 1:4 (~250 mL urine every 4 weeks) | Months 1–7, then stop so the plant fills cormels, not leaves | ~5.7 g N/L → 9–11 g N, matching ~9 g N uptake |
| **Biochar** | **100 g dry**, charged with ~400 mL urine | 2 pockets 20–25 cm from the plant, away from the bone ash | Field trial used 10–30 t/ha (1–3 kg/plant) on degraded sand; our soil is rich in organic matter |
| **Vermicast** | **~300 g** in the planting hole (100 g if short) + ~200 g at earthing-up (months 3–4) | Mixed into the soil | Manure/compost trials: 8–37.5 t/ha. Mature manure vermicompost suppressed *Pythium* (Artavia 2010) |
| **Great White** | ~1 g (+3.6 g bentonite) | Dusted on the planting piece, where new roots touch it | AMF mix gave 2–3× more minitubers in vitro (single study) |
| **Rice husk** | **~500 g (≈4–5 L)** mixed into the hole or ridge | At planting | Kerala: 500 g/plant lowered bulk density, raised porosity and yield; helps drainage against *Pythium* |
| **Mulch** | Thin, ~3 cm, kept 10 cm clear of the plant's base | After planting | See §4 |

**Ca:** the bone ash (~37% Ca) covers the ~2 g uptake.

## 3. Epsom salt at 5 g/L

- **In urine used to charge biochar: yes.** 5 g/L Epsom ≈ 490 mg/L Mg. With aged urine at ~500 mg/L P, the Mg:P molar ratio is ~1.26:1, right at the ~1.2:1 optimum for capturing urine P as struvite (see [urine-epsom-biochar-fertilizer.md](urine-epsom-biochar-fertilizer.md)). Struvite releases P slowly, so the Andisol can't fix it.
- **In side-dress urine: not needed, but harmless.** 2 L × 5 g ≈ 1 g Mg per season (~30% of uptake).
  - The only Mg trial on otoe (Puerto Rico, 1968) found no yield gain and a slightly negative trend.
  - Mg fertilizer pays mainly on low-Mg or acidic soils (Wang 2020 meta-analysis), and Volcán soils are usually high in Mg.
  - Otoe needs a lot of K, and extra Mg competes with K uptake.
- The Caribbean tech pack does add Mg (60 g potassium-magnesium sulfate at 7 months), but on unknown island soils. A Ca/Mg/K soil test settles it.

## 4. Mulch and soil temperature

- Organic mulches raise otoe yield in the lowlands: sawdust at 20 t/ha gave +36–52% (Shiyam 2007), Tithonia at 7.5 t/ha also helped (Agbede 2015), and live and dry mulches raised cormel yield more than corm yield (Amalu 2014).
- **But organic mulch cools the soil**, and Volcán (~18 °C mean) is already otoe's cold limit. Black plastic raised soil temperature by ~2 °C and taro corm yield by 72% in Nigeria (Ezeaku 2007).
- **My inference, not tested for Volcán:** a thin mulch (erosion and splash control) is better than a thick one here, kept off the base to avoid rot.

## 5. Earthing-up

Pull loose soil from between the rows up around the base, making a 10–15 cm mound, at months 3–4 (when cormels start) and optionally at months 5–6. Put the vermicast and that month's SOP down first, then cover them. It covers the cormels (bigger, protected), anchors the tall plant and buries the fertilizer. In the rainy season, don't pile soil against wet petioles, and slope the mound so water runs off.

## 6. Calendar (per plant)

| Month | Do |
|---|---|
| 0 (planting) | Hole: 300 g vermicast + 500 g rice husk + 15–20 g bone ash ring; Great White on the planting piece; 100 g charged biochar in 2 pockets; thin mulch |
| 1 | 5 g SOP; urine ~250 mL (diluted 1:4), then every 4 weeks to month 7 |
| 3 | 5 g SOP |
| 3–4 | 15 g bone ash + ~200 g vermicast; earth up |
| 5 | 5 g SOP; optional second earthing-up (months 5–6) |
| 7 | 5 g SOP; last urine |
| 9–12 | Harvest when lower leaves yellow |

## 7. Gaps

- No otoe trial on urine, bone ash, Great White, vermicast or charged biochar. The doses are conversions.
- The only Mg trial on otoe is from 1968 and on one soil.
- Uptake data come from lowland Puerto Rico. Volcán's cooler, slower crop probably takes up less per month.
- Without a soil test, the K and Mg advice is a best guess.
