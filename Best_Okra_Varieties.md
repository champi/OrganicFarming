## Seed Saving Notes

All varieties above are **open-pollinated** — seeds saved from them will grow true to type **if isolated from other okra varieties**.

Okra flowers are perfect (self-pollinating) but highly attractive to bees. To save pure seed when growing multiple varieties:

- **Isolation distance:** 500 m (1,640 ft) minimum between varieties, or
- **Bag individual flower buds** with fine mesh (organza) bags before they open, remove once the pod begins swelling, or
- **Grow only one variety per year** — okra seeds remain viable 3–5 years

Mark seed pods with ribbon or tape so you don't accidentally harvest and eat them. Let pods dry completely brown and brittle on the plant. One pod yields 50–100 seeds.
