# Bone Ash (P) and SOP (K) Doses for 8 Garden Crops — Volcán Andisol

> **Literature review, Oct 2026.** Per-plant amounts, timing and placement of raw bone ash (phosphorus) and potassium sulfate (SOP, potassium) for pumpkin, climbing bean, corn, papaya, potted lemon, otoe, sweet potato and cherry tomato, with mycorrhizae (AMF) taken into account. Background: [bone-ash-phosphorus-fertilizer.md](bone-ash-phosphorus-fertilizer.md) (§11 AMF, §15 dose rule, §16) and [zucchini-nutrition-guide.md](zucchini-nutrition-guide.md) (§3 P, §4 K).

**Assumptions:** medium soil P and K (no soil test yet), plants inoculated with or growing near AMF, ash at ~16% P, SOP at 50% K₂O. Zucchini is covered in its own trial sheet: 15 g ash once, 3 × 5 g SOP.

---

## 1. Quick Reference

| Crop | Bone ash (+ same weight vermicast) | When (P) | SOP | When (K) | Ring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Corn** | ~25 g per 4-stalk cluster (~6 g per stalk) | At sowing, beside the seed; or before the 6-leaf stage (~4 wk) | ~10 g per cluster (~2.5 g per stalk) | Once, ~4 wk (6-leaf stage) | 10–15 cm around the cluster |
| **Climbing bean** | ~2–3 g per plant ≈ 10–15 g per cluster (on top of the corn dose) | At bean sowing | ~1 g per plant ≈ 5 g per cluster | First flowers | Same cluster ring, off the stems |
| **Pumpkin** | ~50 g | At sowing or within the first 4 wk | ~30 g/season: 3 × 10 g | First flowers, fruit set, +2–3 wk | 30–40 cm from the crown |
| **Cherry tomato** | ~15 g | Transplant to ~30 days after (DAT) | ~25–30 g/season: 5 × 5–6 g | From ~30–35 DAT (first truss), every 2–3 wk | 15–20 cm from the stem |
| **Sweet potato** | ~6–8 g | At planting (skip if >6 wk in and growing well) | ~8–10 g/season: 2 × 4–5 g | At planting + 30–60 days after | Along the ridge |
| **Otoe** | 15–20 g at planting + ~15 g at 3–4 months (established <7 months: one 15–20 g dose now) | Before the cormels fill (month ~6) | ~15 g/season: 3 × 5 g | Months 1, 3, 5 | 20–30 cm from the base |
| **Papaya** | ~50 g every 3–4 months (~150–200 g/yr) | Planting, then through fruiting | ~20 g per month (~240 g/yr; 10 g if soil K is high) | Monthly | Trunk to halfway to the canopy edge |
| **Lemon in a pot** | ~1 g per L of pot, once a year (20 L ≈ 20 g) | Now is fine (flowering) | ~1 g per L of pot per year (20 L: 5 g every 3 months) | From flowering into fruit set | Outer edge of the pot, water through |

## 2. Method

### Bone ash (P)
- **Raw, fine-ground ash** (<250 µm, flour-like), mixed **1:1 by weight with vermicast**.
- **Mix it into a ring of soil**, not a pocket. Pull the mulch back, scratch a trench 5–8 cm deep at the ring distance, sprinkle evenly, stir it through ~1–2 L of soil, re-cover and water.
- **No citric acid needed.** The AMF and their bacteria dissolve the apatite, and slow P doesn't switch the symbiosis off (bone-ash guide §11).
- **Dose rule:** ~2–3× the crop's P uptake (bone-ash guide §15). Lower it for strongly mycorrhizal crops.
- **Repeat crops in the same bed:** about half the dose.

### SOP (K)
- **Apply it dry, straight from the bag.** No need to dissolve it.
  - Dry SOP spread on the soil is the standard field method.
  - In blueberry, one dry spring application of SOP matched weekly dissolved SOP through drip in yield, fruit quality and leaf K [K4].
  - SOP's salt index is low: 42.6, against 116 for potassium chloride (KCl) [K1, K2].
- **Spread it thinly along the same ring and scratch it into the top 1–2 cm.**
  - Keep it 5–10 cm from stems and off wet leaves.
  - Never put it in the seed hole: keep under ~10 lb/acre of N + K₂O in the seed row [K2].
  - Water it in.
- **Split it into small doses.** Volcanic (allophanic) soils have no K-fixing clays, and ~25% of applied K leached [K5]. On high-rainfall volcanic soils, K is split 2–4 times a year [K6].
- **Measuring:** weigh 5 g once to calibrate a spoon. A level teaspoon is roughly 5–8 g depending on how fine the powder is.
- **Rate logic:** as in the zucchini guide, SOP covers ~60–70% of crop K uptake. Urine, vermicast and soil supply the rest.
- **Compatibility:** same day as urine is OK; keep ≥3 days from wood ash.

## 4. Caveats

- **Soil K may already be high.** Cerro Punta farms tested high in K (210–567 mg/L, IDIAP; urine guide). If your soil tests high, halve SOP or skip it.
- **Mg is typically high here.** Extra K competes with Mg and Ca for uptake, so don't over-apply.
- **One soil test** (Modified-Olsen P, exchangeable K/Ca/Mg) would replace the "medium" assumption for every row.
- **Otoe and potted-lemon rates are extrapolations.** There are no bone ash or SOP trials for either.
- **Stage matters.** A crop past its P window (sweet potato >6 wk, corn past the 6-leaf stage) gets a half dose or none.
